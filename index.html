Markdown in. Rich text out.

An AI chat answer, a README, your own notes: copy the Markdown, open Pastedown, and copy it back out as formatted text. Headings, lists, tables and links arrive intact in Apple Notes, Mail, Pages, Word or Google Docs.

Download for Mac Free Version 1.0.1 · macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon · Signed and notarized

One job, done properly

Pasting Markdown into Notes or Mail leaves you with asterisks and pound signs. Pastedown puts the formatting on the clipboard instead.

Pastes well everywhere

The clipboard gets rich text, HTML and plain text at once, so each app takes the form it handles best.

The whole of Markdown

Headings, bold and italic, links, lists, task lists, tables, block quotations and code, as GitHub writes them.

See it before you copy

The source on the left, the result on the right. Fix a heading or delete a paragraph first; the preview follows as you type and as you scroll.

Starts with your clipboard

Open Pastedown and what you last copied is already there. After that it leaves your text alone, unless you ask it to keep following the clipboard.

Or convert in place

With Pastedown open, Convert Clipboard to Rich Text (⌥⌘C) turns the Markdown on the clipboard into rich text without loading it into the window.

Comfortable to read

Five typefaces, zoom, line width, justified text and dark mode: the same reading options as Plainview.

How it works

Three keystrokes from Markdown to a formatted note.

Copy the Markdown

From a chat, an editor, a web page or a file.

Open Pastedown

It loads the clipboard and shows the result. Press ⇧⌘C to copy it as rich text.

Paste

Into Notes, Mail, Pages, Word, Google Docs, or anywhere else that takes formatted text.

Nothing leaves your Mac

No more pasting your text into a converter website. Pastedown converts on your Mac and makes no network connections.

  • No uploads, no network connections
  • No AI
  • No account, no sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or ads
  • Your text isn’t saved anywhere
  • Images in your Markdown aren’t fetched
