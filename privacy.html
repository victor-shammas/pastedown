Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

The short version
  • Pastedown has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads.
  • It makes no network connections at all.
  • It reads the text on your clipboard and converts it on your Mac. The text never leaves your Mac and isn’t saved.
  • It uses no AI service.
  • I (the developer) never receive any of your data.

This policy explains what the Pastedown app for macOS (“Pastedown”, “the app”) reads, what it stores, and what it never does. Pastedown is made by Victor Shammas, an independent developer (“I”, “me”).

1. What Pastedown reads

Pastedown reads the text on your clipboard: once when it opens (unless you turn that off), each time it comes to the front if you have turned on Continuously Auto-Load Clipboard, and whenever you choose Load Clipboard or Convert Clipboard to Rich Text. It reads whatever text is there, so if you have just copied something private, it will appear in the Pastedown window. It reads no files.

When you choose Copy as Rich Text or Convert Clipboard to Rich Text, Pastedown replaces the clipboard with the formatted text.

2. Network use

Pastedown makes no network connections. Markdown can contain links to images and other files on the web; Pastedown doesn’t fetch them. A link you click in the preview, and the Help menu’s items, open in your web browser. If the text you copy out contains an image link, the app you paste it into may load that image.

3. What Pastedown stores on your Mac

  • Your settings: the display options, the clipboard options, and the window’s size and position.

The text in the window isn’t saved; it is gone when you quit. Settings are kept in ~/Library/Preferences/com.victorshammas.pastedown.plist. The preview is drawn by the web view built into macOS, which may keep small housekeeping files of its own in your Library folder; they hold none of your text.

4. What Pastedown never does

  • It never sends your text, or anything about it, anywhere.
  • It has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind, and uses no third-party code that does.

5. Download

Pastedown is downloaded from GitHub, whose privacy statement applies to the download. I don’t receive your name, email address or anything else about you.

6. Email

If you email me for support, I use your message only to answer you, and you can ask me to delete it.

7. Children

Pastedown collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: contact+pastedown@victorshammas.com.
